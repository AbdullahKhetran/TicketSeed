Hi team,

Like Uber but for handymen: leaky tap, stuck door, light fitting — people don't know who to call and fear getting ripped off. Customer describes what's broken; a tradesperson comes. Tradespeople sign up and get jobs.

Core: fast, trustworthy matching. Ratings matter (e.g. 50 five-star reviews). Monetise by taking a cut per job — maybe 15–20%, TBD. Tradespeople must see earnings clearly.

Also needed:
- Photo upload of the problem
- Chat after match
- In-app payments (not cash)
- Accounts for both sides
- Quote before commit
- GPS while the tradesperson is en route
- Reviews/ratings
- Tradesperson sets job types and coverage area

Look: clean/simple; my mum should use it. Mobile-first; web version nice for tradespeople on a laptop.

Launch ~4 months, limited budget, keep v1 simple. Home services UX is still broken — there's a gap.

Any questions just ask!

Danny
